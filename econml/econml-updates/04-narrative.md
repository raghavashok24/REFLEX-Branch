# 4. Narrative fit of the results

## 4.1 The spine, and why it works

The paper has a real narrative spine, which most theory-heavy workshop
submissions lack: one substitution (R for 11'), one quantity (m_N =
N_eff * m_1), four results all stated in it, so every result inherits the
supply-chain parameter "for free," and the two policy levers meet in a
frontier that exists only because Results 1 and 3 share the quantity. The
sentence "The substitution of R for 11' is the whole paper, so it is
derived rather than posited" is the thesis working correctly; keep it.

The lever taxonomy (slow down / correct / diversify / price) gives the
reader a place to stand in every section, and the closing Weitzman gesture
(instruments supplied, choice posed, not answered) is the right amount of
economics for this venue.

## 4.2 Three narrative frictions, with fixes

**1. The dry-run framing self-undermines at one spot.** "It establishes
nothing about a market" is said of five of the six panels, twice. Honesty
is the paper's brand and must stay, but this particular sentence hands the
reviewer their summary sentence ("five of six panels establish nothing").
The distinction the paper actually means is internal vs external validity.
Suggested replacement at the Section 8 occurrence (the Section 8 framing
sentence, not the limitations one, which is fine):

> "it checks that the closed forms were derived from the map we believe
> they were derived from, which is internal validity, and it has caught
> real errors. What it cannot supply is external validity: evidence that a
> market behaves like the map."

Same honesty, no free ammunition. (Not applied in proposed-v5, since it is
a judgment call on voice; the page cost is one line, which the v5 margin
absorbs.)

**2. The reader meets the evidence hierarchy late.** The abstract's last
sentence and the introduction promise measured amplification, and Section 8
then reveals the measured/dry-run split. A reviewer who reads
abstract-figures-conclusion first (most do) can feel the ground shift. One
clause in the introduction's contributions paragraph would fix the
expectation: after "(5) The Pigouvian wedge and the over-adaptation
corollary," add a sentence such as "Evidence discipline is part of the
contribution: each panel carries its evidential status, one measured in a
real order-flow market, five as certified derivation checks." This converts
the split from a discovered weakness into an announced method. Costs two
lines; candidates to pay for it are in `06-writing-style.md`.

**3. Result 4 is the narrative orphan.** Results 1 to 3 form a tight loop
(the object, the unilateral lever, the collective lever, the frontier);
the wedge arrives with new machinery (AR(1) variance, welfare weights) and
no figure reference until the experiments section. Two low-cost stitches:
point forward from the Section 5 free-riding paragraph ("the market
free-rides below the threshold") to the wedge as the instrument that
repairs exactly that under-supply, one clause; and point backward from the
wedge's provenance channel to the diversity floor, which the text already
does well. The forward stitch is the missing one.

## 4.3 Ordering judged sound

Setup before Result 1 (the base result as "inherited scaffolding" with one
object replaced) is the correct order and well executed. Result 2 before 3
is right (unilateral before collective). Experiments as a single section
with the status table, rather than per-result subsections, is right for a
nine-page build. Supervision folded into the close is right; do not give it
back its own section until the estimator is formal (journal version).

## 4.4 The title and abstract as narrative

Title: strong, specific, and honest about both halves (the mechanism and
the economics). Keep.

Abstract: does real work but runs long, and its strongest concrete asset
(the herd-immunity coverage law with its efficacy) is buried mid-paragraph.
If it is trimmed, cut from the front (the first three sentences can be
two), never from the numbers at the back. Optional, not urgent.
